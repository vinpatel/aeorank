`ai.txt` declares how AI systems may use your content (training vs retrieval) and which crawlers you allow.

## What it is

A plain-text policy file generated from the scan. It is one of the **9 files** `generateFiles()` writes — not a stub.

## Why it matters

The `content-licensing` dimension looks for a real `/ai.txt` on your site. Deploying this file is how you score that check. It does not track citations.

## How to deploy

Place `ai.txt` at the site root (`https://your-site.com/ai.txt`).
