Hard prohibitions:
- Do NOT remove `astro` from `packages/astro` — `src/index.ts` has
  `import type { AstroIntegration } from "astro"`, a real import.
- Do NOT remove `@nuxt/kit` or `h3` from `packages/nuxt` — both are imported.
- Do NOT touch any `peerDependencies` block anywhere. Peers are the consumer contract and
  do not cause installs.
- Do NOT treat `tsup.config.ts` `external:` entries as evidence of a dependency — they are
  bundler directives and do not require the package to be installed. This is exactly why
  `next`, `@remix-run/*`, `@sveltejs/kit`, and `@docusaurus/types` are removable.
